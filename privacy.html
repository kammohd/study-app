Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

AI Study is a small personal study app. This page explains what it stores about you.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email address and name, used only to sign you in.
  • Your study data: topics ticked off, weekly goals, quiz attempts, and how many AI requests you have used today.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, sync your progress across your devices, and apply a daily AI-usage limit. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Where it is stored

Sign-in and data are handled by Supabase, and the app server runs on Google Cloud. Access is restricted to invited users.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and all of its data to be deleted at any time by opening an issue at github.com/kammohd/study-app/issues.
