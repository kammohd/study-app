Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 (activity summary for the administrator added)

AI Study is a small personal study app. This page explains what it stores about you.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email address and name, used only to sign you in.
  • Your study data: topics ticked off, weekly goals, quiz attempts, and how many AI requests you have used today.
  • When you open the app signed in: the time you were last active, so the administrator can see who is using it.
  • If you turn on notifications: a code that identifies your device with the browser's push service, your timezone, and a one-line summary of which weekly goals are still open, used to time and write your reminders.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, sync your progress across your devices, send the reminders you asked for, and apply a daily AI-usage limit. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

AI features (quiz questions, coding challenges, hints, grading, reminders and the daily quote) are written by Google's Gemini service. When you use them, the topic text, your answer or code, or your goals summary is sent to Gemini to produce the result. Your email address and name are not sent.

Where it is stored

Sign-in and data are handled by Supabase, and the app server runs on Google Cloud. Reminders are delivered through your browser's own push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft). Access is restricted to invited users. Other users cannot read your data. The app's administrator (the person who runs the invite list) can see a summary of each invited person's activity: your sign-in email, lessons and topics completed this week, goals met, study streak, quiz accuracy, coding challenges solved, AI requests used today, and when you were last active. The administrator cannot see your answers or code. You can turn notifications off on a device at any time from the Me tab.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and all of its data to be deleted at any time by opening an issue at github.com/kammohd/study-app/issues.
